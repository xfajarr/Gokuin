# Logo prompt

Paste into an image model. It describes the project and the one historical object
the name comes from, and leaves the design decisions open rather than dictating a
shape.

---

```
Design a logo mark for a project called Gokuin.

What the project is:

Gokuin is an independent testing body for Ethereum transaction routes. When you
send a transaction, you can send it through services that promise it will stay
private and will not be front-run. Those services measure their own performance
and publish their own numbers. Nobody independent checks them. Gokuin sends real
test transactions through each route, measures what actually happened, and
publishes a permanent public record of whether the promise held.

The name is Japanese. 極印, gokuin, is the mark an assayer strikes into precious
metal to certify its real purity, as opposed to its claimed purity. The same word
carries a second sense: to be gokuin-stamped is to be branded permanently, with a
record that cannot be lifted off afterwards. Both meanings are the product. The
project is the assay and the stamp.

Its closest historical ancestor is the British proof house, which since 1637 has
been legally required to test-fire every firearm with a deliberately overcharged
round before it can be sold, and then stamp it. The manufacturer is forbidden from
testing its own.

So the mark should feel like: something struck rather than drawn. A punch into
metal rather than ink on paper. Evidence of a test having been performed. An
institution rather than a product. Old, exacting, slightly severe. It certifies;
it does not advertise.

Constraints:

Monochrome. One mark, no wordmark, no tagline.
It must survive at 16 by 16 pixels and at poster size.
Geometric and struck, not illustrative. No gradients, no glow, no 3D bevel, no
depth shading, no drop shadows.
No generic crypto or tech visual language: no cubes, no chain links, no shields,
no hexagons, no circuit traces, no abstract swooshes, no blockchain nodes.
No Japanese characters, and no attempt at a calligraphic brushstroke.
Flat vector. Solid black on an off-white ground.
```

---

**If you want variations, vary one thing at a time.** Ask for the same brief with
a different emphasis rather than rewriting the whole prompt: "lean harder on the
punch-into-metal reading", or "lean harder on the assayer's touchstone reading",
or "read it as an institutional seal rather than a maker's mark".

**What to reject.** Anything that reads as a generic tech logo, anything with a
gradient, anything that needs colour to work, and anything that looks like it was
drawn rather than stamped. The test is whether it still reads at 16 pixels in a
browser tab, and whether it looks like it certifies something.
